## ⭐ Capstone: Where Do Medicare Claim Costs Come From?
**[→ View the case study](capstone/medicare-claims-cost-analysis/README.md)**

A health plan cost analysis of **138,556 Medicare members and 558,211 claims**, built in **BigQuery (SQL)** and **Power BI**, modeled on the work of a regional health plan's data analysts.

**Key findings**
- 5% of members drive **43.6%** of total cost
- Hospital stays are **7.3%** of claims but **73.4%** of cost
- Members with 5+ chronic conditions are **37%** of members but **~65%** of cost
- Readmissions rise from **0.4%** to **10.6%** as chronic conditions increase

## Why healthcare (and rural health)
Health plans rely on data analysts to study claims, hospital, enrollment and cost data for finance, quality and care-management teams. In rural communities, where providers are scarce and travel distances are long, this work is especially important: better use of data can help keep members with chronic conditions healthy and out of the hospital. This repository documents my learning and a self-directed project built to practice those skills with public data.
