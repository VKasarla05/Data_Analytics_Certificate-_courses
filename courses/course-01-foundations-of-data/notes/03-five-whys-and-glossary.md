# The Five Whys: Root Cause Analysis

## Concept
A root cause is the underlying reason a problem occurs. The Five Whys technique finds it
by asking "why" repeatedly (typically five times), each answer prompting the next
question, until you reach the actual source of the problem rather than a symptom of it.

**Why it's useful:**
- Simple and fast — no special tooling required.
- Collaborative — a good excuse to talk to colleagues in other parts of the business.
- Domain-agnostic — works for any industry or problem type.

**When I'll use it:** whenever a metric looks off and the first explanation feels too
easy — e.g., "readmissions are up" → "why?" → keep drilling instead of stopping at the
first plausible answer.
