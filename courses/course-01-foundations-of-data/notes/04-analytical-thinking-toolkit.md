# Analytical Thinking Toolkit

## Core data analytics skills
- **Curiosity** — pushing to see how much insight can be pulled from the data, expected or not.
- **Understanding of context** — narrows down which variables actually matter, leading to more meaningful insights.
- **Technical mindset** — approaching problems and datasets systematically, starting with how data is cleaned and prepared.
- **Data design** — how information is organized; an extension of technical mindset.
- **Data strategy** — the overall approach to managing people, process, and tools for a given analysis.

## Definition
Analytical thinking = identifying and defining a problem, then solving it with data in an
organized, step-by-step way.

## Five key aspects of analytical thinking
1. Visualization
2. Strategy
3. Problem-orientation
4. Correlation
5. Big-picture and detail-oriented thinking

## My takeaway
Curiosity and context are the two that are easiest to skip under time pressure but matter
most for catching a wrong or shallow conclusion — worth deliberately slowing down for on
every project, including the applied case study.
