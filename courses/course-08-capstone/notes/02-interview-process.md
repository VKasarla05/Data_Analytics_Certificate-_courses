# The Interview Process

## Typical stages
1. **Recruiter / HR screen:** background, interest, logistics
2. **Hiring manager interview:** experience, motivation, fit
3. **Technical assessment:** SQL, spreadsheets, a case study or take-home task
4. **Panel / final interview:** team members, presentation of work

## Preparing
- Research the company, its members/customers, and the role
- Prepare **STAR** stories: Situation, Task, Action, Result
- Practice explaining a project in 2 minutes: problem → data → method → finding → impact
- Be ready to walk through SQL and explain every step
- Prepare questions to ask the interviewer

## Technical interview tips
- Clarify the question before solving
- Think out loud
- Check your results (row counts, totals)
- State assumptions and limitations

## Tools
- **Google Interview Warmup:** practice answering common questions
- Mock interviews with peers

## My 2-minute capstone pitch
"I analyzed 138,556 Medicare members and 558,211 claims in BigQuery. After rejecting a first dataset that failed credibility checks, I cleaned and reconciled the data to the dollar, then found that 5% of members drove 44% of cost, hospital stays were 7% of claims but 73% of dollars, and members with five or more chronic conditions were 37% of members but 65% of cost. I built a Power BI dashboard and recommended targeting care coordination and post-discharge follow-up for that group."
