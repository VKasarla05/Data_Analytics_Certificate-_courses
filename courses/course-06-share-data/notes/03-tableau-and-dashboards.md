# Tableau & Dashboards

## Tableau basics
- **Tableau Public:** free; work is saved publicly online (good for portfolios)
- **Data source page:** connect to files (CSV, Excel, Google Sheets)
- **Dimensions** (categories, blue) vs. **measures** (numbers, green)
- **Shelves:** Rows, Columns, Filters, Pages
- **Marks card:** Color, Size, Label, Detail, Tooltip
- **Show Me:** suggests chart types for the selected fields
- **Dual axis:** two measures on one chart (e.g., bars + line)
- **Calculated fields:** new fields from formulas

## Dashboards
A dashboard is a single view combining several visuals that update together.

**Good dashboards:**
- answer the stakeholders' key questions at a glance
- put the most important metric or chart top-left / largest
- use consistent colors and fonts
- include filters only when they help the audience

**Types:** strategic (long-term, executives), operational (short-term, day-to-day), analytical (detailed, for analysts)

## Filters
- **Data source filter:** limits what's loaded
- **Worksheet filter:** one chart
- **Dashboard filter / action:** applies across visuals

## Tableau Story
A sequence of sheets or dashboards ("story points") that walks the audience through the narrative step by step.
