# Phase 1: Ask

## Scenario
Acting as a data analyst supporting the finance/quality team of a regional Medicaid-focused
health plan (modeled on UPHP), responding to a stakeholder request.

## Stakeholder question
"Which member segments are driving the highest per-member cost, and are there utilization
patterns (e.g., ER vs. primary care) that suggest opportunities to improve cost and access
to care?"

## Problem statement
The plan wants to understand cost drivers across its member population so it can target
outreach or care-coordination programs at the segments with the highest avoidable cost.

## Guiding questions
1. Which age group / plan type / diagnosis category has the highest average cost per claim?
2. Are there segments with disproportionately high ER utilization relative to primary care?
3. Is there a correlation between claim volume and claim cost within any segment?
4. What would a plausible root cause be for the highest-cost segment (Five Whys)?
5. What's one concrete, actionable recommendation this analysis could support?

## Success criteria
- A clear, one-page summary a non-technical stakeholder (e.g., a claims or finance
  director) could act on.
- At least one visualization showing cost by segment.
- One specific, defensible recommendation tied to the data.
