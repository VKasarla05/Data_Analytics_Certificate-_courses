## What I learned
- The difference between data-driven and data-inspired decisions, and how incomplete
  data (New Coke) or miscommunication (Mars Climate Orbiter) can derail a "correct" analysis.
- Qualitative vs. quantitative data, and when to reach for spreadsheets vs. Tableau.
- Spreadsheet fundamentals: functions vs. formulas, relative/absolute/mixed references,
  and how to read and prevent common errors (`#REF!`, `#VALUE!`, etc.).
- The Who/What/Where/When/Why/How framework for giving data context.
- How to communicate with different stakeholder groups (executive, customer-facing,
  data science) and structure updates around the four audience questions.
