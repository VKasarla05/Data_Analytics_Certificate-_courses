## What I learned
- The considerations behind choosing what data to collect: source type (first/second/
  third-party), format (structured vs. unstructured, wide vs. long), and whether it
  actually answers the business question.
- Relational database basics: primary/foreign keys, normalization.
- SQL fundamentals (`SELECT`/`FROM`/`WHERE`) and BigQuery-specific quirks (case sensitivity).
- What metadata is and why it makes data reliable and consistent.
- Sorting and filtering as core data-cleaning techniques, and how to fill missing values
  and convert text categories to numeric codes.
- File naming and organization best practices.
